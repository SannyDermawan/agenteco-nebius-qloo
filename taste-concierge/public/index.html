Taste Concierge

Describe what you like. Recommendations come from the Qloo taste graph and are explained by NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory. An AgentEco seller agent, paid through escrow on a test network.

How it works

1. A small Nemotron model reads your request. 2. Qloo resolves what you already like and returns similar entities. 3. A larger Nemotron model picks and explains. 4. The agent checks every pick against what Qloo returned, so it cannot recommend something Qloo did not give it.
